# Sisyphus and His Big Rock

Sisyphus push big rock up hill. Rock roll back down. Sisyphus push rock again. Rock roll back down again.

This is the way.

## The Rock

Big rock. Very big. Sisyphus not complain. Sisyphus just push.

## The Hill

Very steep. Very long. No top in sight. Sisyphus push anyway.

## Conclusion

Rock come back down. Sisyphus push again. Forever.
